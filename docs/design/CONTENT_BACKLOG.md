# Mutation Meddley Content Backlog

This backlog is intentionally broader than the current milestone. It is a source of future mutation concepts and branch identities, not a commitment to implement everything immediately.

## Near-term candidates

### Too Many Teeth

Class: BASIC / ABERRANT

Core loop:

- teeth appear in places where teeth should not be
- bite/contact mechanics expand with body-plan state
- equipment and limb configuration can change the mutation's value

Potential rank-3 identities:

- Maw: direct predatory biting and penetration
- Dermal Dentition: retaliation/contact damage
- Feeding Array: recovery or resource extraction from successful bites

Potential hidden ideas:

- Multiple Arms + BODY_PLAN interaction: dental growth on hands/limbs
- corrosive or venomous gland synergy

### Colonial Organism

Class: KEYSTONE / SYMBIOTIC / BODY_PLAN

Core loop:

- the character becomes an ecology rather than a single organism
- biomass, replacement tissues, detachable organisms, and distributed survival

Identity candidates:

- Distributed Body: resilience and redundant organs
- Brood Organism: deployable biological helpers
- Parasitic Colony: resource theft and enemy infestation

Tradeoffs:

- food demand
- fire/chemical vulnerabilities
- equipment/body-slot pressure

### Wrong Geometry

Class: KEYSTONE / ABERRANT / DIMENSIONAL

Core loop:

- adjacency and physical occupation become unreliable

Identity candidates:

- Folded Reach: attack around or through geometry
- Misplaced Body: defensive displacement and partial non-occupation
- Interior Distance: movement and storage-like spatial tricks

Tradeoffs:

- spatial backlash
- unpredictable vulnerability to dimensional/phase effects

### Borrowed Tomorrow

Class: ABERRANT / TEMPORAL

Core loop:

- gain power or actions now by creating future debt

Identity candidates:

- Action Debt: borrow action economy
- Cooldown Mortgage: immediately refresh abilities at later cost
- Wound Deferral: postpone damage or debuffs

Tradeoffs:

- debt must always be paid in a legible way
- no infinite loops with cooldown/action systems

### Neural Lichen

Class: SYMBIOTIC / PHOTOSYNTHETIC / PSIONIC

Core loop:

- light and mental state feed a lichen integrated with the nervous system

Identity candidates:

- Sunmind: cognition and recovery in strong light
- Dream Moss: psychic defense and altered sleep/rest loops
- Signal Garden: perception and low-grade telepathic sensing

### Gut Oracle

Class: SYMBIOTIC / METABOLIC / PROBABILISTIC

Core loop:

- diet creates predictive or probabilistic effects

Identity candidates:

- Omen Fermentation: meals generate short-lived foresight
- Hazard Appetite: consuming dangerous substances reveals or resists threats
- Prophetic Microbiome: rare warnings or route information tied to digestion

The mechanic must remain comprehensible and should not become free omniscience.

## Interaction-first concept seeds

These are not full mutation pitches yet. Each begins with a Qud system interaction that could produce a mutation.

- liquid surface tension manipulation
- blood chemistry that reacts with specific fluids
- body heat used as a currency
- armor that grows differently after penetration failures versus successful penetrations
- mutation that learns from repeated damage types
- mutation that modifies recoil/knockback as locomotion
- mutation that uses darkness as stored mass or cover
- mutation that grows temporary organs based on recent diet
- mutation that treats fungal infection as equipment-like modularity
- mutation that allows limited biological interfacing with robots
- mutation that turns reputation or social proximity into a psychic resource
- mutation whose body parts change phase independently
- mutation that creates delayed echoes of movement or attacks

## Backlog priority rubric

Prioritize concepts that score well on:

1. distinct play pattern
2. interaction with existing Qud systems
3. branch diversity
4. controller-friendly usability
5. technical feasibility without invasive patches
6. save/load safety
7. synergy potential
8. understandable tradeoffs
9. thematic weirdness
10. testability

Do not prioritize a concept merely because it is easy to implement if it adds no meaningful decisions.
